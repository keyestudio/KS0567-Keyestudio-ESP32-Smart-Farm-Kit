2. Software Installation
========================

.. _21-install-driver:

2.1 Install Driver
------------------

**NOTE: If your computer has already installed a driver, please just
skip this section. If not, please follow these steps.**

Click |image1| to select “ **Install driver** ”.

|image2|

- Click “ **Next** ” when you see the device driver installation wizard:

|image3|

- Click “ **Finish** ”.

|image4|

- Click “ **Next** ”.

|image5|

- Click “ **Finish** ”.

|image6|

- If a warning pops up, just click “ **Allow** ”. Then click “
  **Install** ”.

|image7|

- Tap “ **Finish** ”.

|image8|

- Click “ **Extract** ”.

|image9|

- Click “ **Next** ”.

|image10|

- Tick “ **I accept this agreement** ” and click “ **Next** ”.

|image11|

- Click “ **Finish** ”.

|image12|

- Choose “ **INSTALL** ”.

|image13|

- A couple of seconds later, the driver will be successfully installed.
  Then click “ **OK** ”.
