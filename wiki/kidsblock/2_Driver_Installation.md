## 2. Software Installation


### 2.1 Install Driver


**NOTE: If your computer has already installed a driver, please just skip this section. If not, please follow these steps.** 

Click ![image4](media/an13.png) to select “ **Install driver** ”.

![img](media/an14.png)

-  Click “ **Next** ” when you see the device driver installation wizard:

![img](media/an15.png)

-  Click “ **Finish** ”.

![img](media/an16.png)

-  Click “ **Next** ”.

![img](media/an17.png)

-  Click “ **Finish** ”.

![img](media/an18.png)

-  If a warning pops up, just click “ **Allow** ”. Then click “ **Install** ”.

![img](media/an19.png)

-  Tap “ **Finish** ”.

![img](media/an20.png)

-  Click “ **Extract** ”.

![img](media/an21.png)

-  Click “ **Next** ”.

![img](media/an22.png)

-  Tick “ **I accept this agreement** ” and click “ **Next** ”.

![img](media/an23.png)

-  Click “ **Finish** ”.

![img](media/an24.png)

-  Choose “ **INSTALL** ”.

![img](media/an25.png)

-  A couple of seconds later, the driver will be successfully installed. Then click “ **OK** ”.

![img](media/an26.png)
